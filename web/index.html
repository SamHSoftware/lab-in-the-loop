Lab in the loop

The lab has blue, round cells with small nuclei and wants green, square cells with big nuclei. Ten compounds have unknown effects. A small neural net learns from each experiment and chooses the next one.

The model runs in your browser with Python (Pyodide). Load it first.

Why a model?

Concentrations→ Lab (cells)→ Phenotype

✗ No gradients. You cannot backpropagate through a real lab.

Concentrations⇠ Neural net (surrogate)⇠ Predicted error

✓ The net imitates the lab and is differentiable, so gradients flow back to the concentrations.

The net learns the lab from past experiments (step 3). With its weights frozen, gradient descent through the net changes the concentrations (step 4). The lab then tests them, which corrects the net.
